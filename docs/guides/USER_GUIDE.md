# Project NEXUS — Community Member User Guide

Welcome to **Project NEXUS**, a community time-banking platform where you exchange services with your neighbours using time credits. One hour of help you give always equals one time credit — everyone's time is valued equally.

This guide walks you through everything a new member needs to know: signing up, finding services, exchanging help, and staying connected with your community.

## What Is Time Banking?

Time banking is a community exchange system where members help each other using **time credits** as currency.

| Concept | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Earning credits** | You offer a service (e.g. gardening, tutoring, a lift to the shops). When the exchange is complete, the hours are transferred to your wallet. |
| **Spending credits** | You use your credits to request a service from another member. |
| **Equal value** | One hour always equals one time credit — a plumber's hour equals a musician's hour. |
| **No money changes hands** | The platform runs entirely on time, not money. |

## Creating Your Account

### Before You Begin

You will need:
- An email address
- Your name and a rough location (town or city is fine)
- A phone number (optional but recommended)

### Registration Steps

Open your community's NEXUS platform in a web browser and tap or click **Sign Up**.

Registration takes about two minutes and walks you through four steps:

**Step 1 — Choose Your Community**

Select the timebank or community you want to join from the list. If your community is not listed, ask your community coordinator for the correct link.

> If you are registering as an **organisation** (a business, club, or charity), select "Organisation" at this step. Otherwise leave it as "Individual".

**Step 2 — Your Details**

Fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| First name | Your real first name or preferred name |
| Last name | Used on your public profile |
| Location | Your town, city, or postcode — used to match you with nearby services |
| Phone number | International format, e.g. `+1 555 123 4567` (optional) |

**Step 3 — Account Setup**

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Email address | Used to log in and receive notifications |
| Password | Minimum 8 characters; include a number or symbol for strength |
| Confirm password | Must match exactly |

You can also sign up with a social account (Google, etc.) if your community has this enabled.

**Step 4 — Terms and Consent**

Read and accept the community terms of service. You must accept to continue.

After you submit, you will receive a **verification email**. Click the link in that email to activate your account before logging in.

## Setting Up Your Profile

After your first login, NEXUS walks you through a short profile setup wizard. A good profile builds trust and helps others decide whether to exchange with you.

### Profile Photo

Upload a clear photo of yourself (or your organisation's logo). Tap the camera icon or drag an image onto the upload area. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP.

### Bio

Write a short description — who you are, what you enjoy, and what you can offer or need help with. A warm, personal bio gets far more responses than a blank profile.

> **Minimum length:** about 10 characters — but aim for a few sentences.

### Interests

Select the categories that interest you (e.g. Gardening, Technology, Arts & Crafts, Transport). This helps NEXUS surface relevant listings and events for you.

### Skills

Mark which categories you can offer help in, and which you need help with. Your listings are pre-populated from this step.

> Some steps are optional — you can tap **Skip** and update them later from **Settings**.

## Exploring the Community Feed

The **Feed** is your community's public notice board. It shows posts, announcements, photos, and polls from other members.

### Reading Posts

Scroll down to browse. Tap any post to open it in full, see comments, and react.

### Reacting and Commenting

- Tap the **heart** or reaction icon to react to a post.
- Tap the **comment icon** to leave a reply.

### Creating a Post

1. Tap the **+ Create Post** button (or the text box at the top of the feed).
2. Write your update, question, or announcement.
3. Optionally attach a photo or add a poll.
4. Tap **Post**.

Your post appears on the feed immediately and is visible to all members of your community.

### Polls

If you want community input on a decision, add a poll to your post. Tap **Add Poll**, enter your options, and post. Members vote directly from the feed.

## Listings — Offers and Requests

Listings are the heart of time banking. A **listing** is either:

- An **offer** — something you can do for others (e.g. "I can help with dog walking on weekday mornings")
- A **request** — something you need help with (e.g. "I'm looking for someone to help me move a sofa")

### Browsing Listings

From the navigation menu, tap **Listings**.

- Use the **search bar** to find listings by keyword.
- Filter by **category** (e.g. Transport, Gardening, IT Help).
- Filter by **type** (Offers / Requests).
- Use the **proximity filter** to show only listings near you.

Tap a listing card to read the full description and see the member who posted it.

### Creating a Listing

1. Go to **Listings** and tap **+ New Listing**.
2. Fill in the form:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Title | Short and descriptive, e.g. "Maths tutoring for secondary school students" |
| Type | Choose **Offer** or **Request** |
| Category | Select the closest match from the list |
| Description | Explain what you can offer or what you need in detail |
| Hours estimate | Approximate hours per session |
| Service type | In-person, remote, or both |
| Location | Where the service takes place (can be your town or an exact address) |
| Skills / tags | Optional keywords to help with search |

3. Tap **Save Listing**.

Your listing is now live and visible to the community.

### Editing or Deactivating a Listing

Open the listing and tap **Edit**. You can update any details or deactivate the listing to hide it temporarily without deleting it.

## Requesting a Service Exchange

An **exchange** is the formal transaction that moves time credits from one member to another. Here is how it works:

### Step 1 — Find a Listing

Browse listings until you find one that meets your need. Open it to read the full description.

### Step 2 — Request the Exchange

Tap the **Request Exchange** button on the listing page.

Fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Proposed hours | How many hours you expect the service to take |
| Message | Introduce yourself and describe your specific need |

Tap **Send Request**.

The listing owner will receive a notification and can accept or decline your request.

### Step 3 — Coordinate

Once your request is accepted, use the **Messages** section to agree on timing, location, and any other details.

### Step 4 — Complete the Exchange

After the service is done:

1. Either party opens the exchange from **My Exchanges**.
2. The service provider marks it as **complete** and confirms the hours.
3. The requester reviews and agrees.
4. Credits are automatically transferred from the requester's wallet to the service provider's wallet.

Both members can leave a review after the exchange is complete.

> **Broker note:** On some communities, exchanges are moderated by a community broker who facilitates the time-credit transfer. You may see your exchange listed as "pending broker review" until they confirm it.

## Your Time-Credit Wallet

Your **Wallet** tracks your time-credit balance and all transactions.

### Viewing Your Balance

Go to **Wallet** from the navigation menu. Your current balance shows at the top of the page.

### Transaction History

Scroll down to see all your past transactions:

| Transaction type | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Received** | You earned credits (completed an exchange as the service provider) |
| **Sent** | You spent credits (received a service) |
| **Pending** | Exchange is awaiting completion or broker review |

Use the filter tabs (All / Earned / Spent / Pending) to narrow the list.

### Transferring Credits

You can send credits directly to another member (for donations or goodwill gestures):

1. Tap **Send Credits** in the Wallet.
2. Search for the member by name.
3. Enter the amount and an optional message.
4. Confirm.

### Community Fund

Some communities have a **Community Fund** — a shared pool that members can donate to and request from for community projects. If your community has one, you'll see it on the Wallet page.

### Exporting Transactions

Tap the **Export** button on the Wallet page to download a CSV file of your transaction history.

## Events

Community events are a great way to meet members in person or online.

### Browsing Events

Go to **Events** from the navigation menu. You will see upcoming events sorted by date.

- Use the **search bar** and **category filter** to narrow results.
- Switch between **List view** and **Map view** to find events near you.

### Viewing an Event

Tap an event card to open it. You'll see:
- Date, time, and location
- A description of what to expect
- The number of attendees

### RSVPing

On the event detail page, tap **Attend** (or the equivalent RSVP button) to register your attendance. You may receive a reminder notification before the event.

### Creating an Event

Community members can create events:

1. On the Events page, tap **+ New Event**.
2. Fill in the title, description, date, time, and location.
3. Set a capacity limit if needed.
4. Tap **Create Event**.

Your event is immediately visible on the community calendar.

## Groups

**Groups** are spaces where members with shared interests or goals can connect and post.

### Finding Groups

Go to **Groups** from the navigation menu. Browse the list or search by name or topic.

### Joining a Group

Tap a group to view it. If it's an open group, tap **Join**. Some groups are moderated — your request will be reviewed by the group administrator.

### Participating in a Group

Inside a group you can:
- Read the group feed and post updates
- Comment on other members' posts
- See who else is in the group

### Creating a Group

Any member can start a group:

1. On the Groups page, tap **+ New Group**.
2. Give it a name and description.
3. Choose **Public** (open to all) or **Private** (invite/approval only).
4. Tap **Create Group**.

As the group creator, you are automatically the administrator and can manage membership.

## Messages

**Messages** lets you have private conversations with other members.

### Starting a Conversation

You can start a conversation from:

- A member's profile page — tap **Message**
- A listing page — tap **Message the Member**
- The Messages section — tap the **compose icon** and search for a member

### Sending Messages

Type your message in the text field at the bottom and tap the send button. You can also share listings, images, or files inside a conversation.

### Notifications

You'll receive a notification (bell icon, or push notification if you allowed them) whenever you receive a new message.

## Changing Your Language

NEXUS supports multiple languages. The platform is available in:

- English
- Irish (Gaeilge)
- Arabic (عربي)
- German (Deutsch)
- French (Français)
- Italian (Italiano)
- Portuguese (Português)
- Spanish (Español)

### How to Change Language

1. Go to **Settings** from your profile menu (tap your avatar in the top right).
2. Open the **Translation** tab (or look for the "Language" option in Profile settings).
3. Select your preferred language from the list.
4. The interface switches immediately — no page reload needed.

> **Note:** Content written by other members (listing descriptions, posts, messages) is in the language the author used. The interface labels and navigation are translated.

## Settings

Access **Settings** from the navigation menu or your profile avatar.

Settings are organised into tabs:

| Tab | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Name, bio, location, avatar, profile type, phone |
| **Notifications** | Email and push notification preferences per category |
| **Privacy** | Profile visibility, search indexing, who can contact you |
| **Security** | Password, two-factor authentication (2FA), active sessions |
| **Skills** | The skill tags shown on your profile |
| **Availability** | Your weekly availability grid (so members know when you're free) |
| **Translation** | Interface language |

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Under the **Security** tab, you can enable 2FA using an authenticator app (e.g. Google Authenticator). This adds an extra verification step at login to protect your account.

### Passkeys (Passwordless Login)

Under **Security**, if your device supports it, you can register a **passkey** (using your device fingerprint, face ID, or Windows Hello). Once registered, you can log in without typing a password.

### Data Export

Under the **Privacy** tab, tap **Export My Data** to download a copy of your account data.

### Deleting Your Account

Under the **Security** tab, scroll to the bottom for the account deletion option. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I registered but haven't received a verification email. What do I do?**  
A: Check your spam or junk folder. If it's not there, return to the login page and look for a "Resend verification email" option. Allow a few minutes for the email to arrive.

**Q: How do I get my first time credits if I start with zero?**  
A: Your community may give new members a small starting balance as a welcome credit. If not, post a listing offering something you can do — once someone completes an exchange with you, credits will appear in your wallet.

**Q: Can I use NEXUS on my phone?**  
A: Yes. The platform works in any mobile browser. Your community may also have a native app (iOS and Android) — ask your community coordinator for the app link.

**Q: What if I have a dispute about an exchange?**  
A: Contact your community administrator or broker through the Messages section. They can review the exchange and mediate if needed.

**Q: Is my location visible to other members?**  
A: Your location is shown at the town or city level by default. You can adjust visibility under **Settings → Privacy**.

**Q: Can I belong to more than one community?**  
A: Yes. If your email address is used across multiple NEXUS communities (tenants), you can switch between them. Each community has its own wallet and listings.

**Q: What does the NEXUS Score mean?**  
A: Your NEXUS Score reflects your activity, exchanges, reviews, and contributions to the community. A higher score builds trust with other members. It is not a financial measure — it has no cash value.

**Q: How do I report inappropriate content or a problem member?**  
A: On any post, listing, or profile you can tap the **three-dot menu (⋯)** or **flag icon** to report it. Your report goes to the community administrator.

**Q: I posted a listing but nobody has responded. What can I do?**  
A: Make sure your listing title and description are clear and specific. Post a community feed update linking to your listing. Joining relevant groups and being active on the platform also increases your visibility.

**Q: Can I volunteer without using time credits?**  
A: Yes. The **Volunteering** section lets you log volunteer hours for community projects without any time-credit exchange involved.
